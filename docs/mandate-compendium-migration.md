# Mandate → Compendium migration

The TribeNet Mandate is being migrated into the Compendium in five reviewable batches. Each completed batch includes exact Mandate text for reference pop-ups, browsable Compendium rule articles, search integration, and cross-links to relevant Compendium entries.

| Batch | Scope | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | §§1–12 — Core game & rules framework | Complete |
| 2 | §§13–14 — Activities & villages | Complete |
| 3 | §§15–19 — Trade, scouting & combat | Complete |
| 4 | §§20–27 — Naval & advanced systems | Pending |
| 5 | §§28–35 + Appendix A — Remaining references & final audit | Pending |

## Batch 1

Source: TribeNet Mandate TN3 Revision N02.2 (16 July 2026).

Batch 1 indexes 119 headings and subheadings from §§1–12.

## Batch 2

Source: TribeNet Mandate TN3 Revision N02.2 (16 July 2026).

Batch 2 indexes 87 headings and subheadings from §§13–14, covering general activities, skill-specific activity rules, uncoded tools and auxiliaries, and village systems through joint projects and research. Compendium skill pages also surface tools, goods and structures mentioned in completed Mandate rules connected to that skill.

## Batch 3

Source: TribeNet Mandate TN3 Revision N02.2 (16 July 2026).

Batch 3 indexes 78 headings and subheadings from §§15–19, covering the Fair and trading, Warrior activities, scouting and raiding, battlefield combat, siege and assault rules, and village defence under siege. Cross-links from these rules flow into relevant skills, items, tools, structures and other Compendium entries using the same completed-Mandate indexing used by earlier batches.

The Compendium displays migration progress as 3/5 and exposes 284 indexed Mandate sections across §§1–19. §20 and later are intentionally withheld until their corresponding batch is migrated and reviewed.
