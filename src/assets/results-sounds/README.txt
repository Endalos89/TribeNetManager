The original UI OGG files in this folder are from Robin Lamb's OpenGameArt “UI
Sound Effects (Button Clicks, User Feedback, Notifications)” pack. The pack is
released under a CC0 / public-domain dedication:
https://opengameart.org/content/ui-sound-effects-button-clicks-user-feedback-notifications

Additional short cues are trimmed from these CC0 sources:

- Butcher: “chopping-lettuce.wav” by krissyeliot, Freesound sound 125521
  https://freesound.org/people/krissyeliot/sounds/125521/
- Horse: “Horse Gallop - Loopable.mp3” by HebronTheatre, Freesound sound 197212
  https://freesound.org/people/HebronTheatre/sounds/197212/
- Wood: “Woodcutting” by fvcalderan, OpenGameArt
  https://opengameart.org/content/woodcutting
- Crafting: a short filtered trim of the CC0 UI Sound Effects source above
- Footsteps: “Different steps on wood, stone, leaves, gravel and mud” by TinyWorlds, OpenGameArt
  https://opengameart.org/content/different-steps-on-wood-stone-leaves-gravel-and-mud

They are used as short, optional cues for results playback. The app keeps a
silent/procedural fallback for systems that block local audio playback.

The hunting.ogg and scouting.ogg cues are short, filtered trims derived from
the CC0 Footsteps and UI Sound Effects sources above. They are not AI-generated
and are kept separate so hunting and scouting do not reuse the movement cue.
